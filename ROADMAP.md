# Learning Roadmap

This is a flexible map rather than a course that must be completed in order.

## Model Internals

Tokenizer, embeddings, attention, causal masking, Transformer blocks, RoPE, RMSNorm, SwiGLU, sampling, decoding, and KV cache.

## Training

Pretraining objectives, data quality, training stability, SFT, LoRA, QLoRA, preference optimization, RL, and reasoning training.

## Evaluation

Benchmark design, generation quality, LLM-as-a-judge, data contamination, reward hacking, and agent evaluation.

## Systems

Inference latency, throughput, quantization, batching, serving, cost accounting, and reproducibility.

## Applications

RAG, tool calling, agents, long context, and multimodal models.
